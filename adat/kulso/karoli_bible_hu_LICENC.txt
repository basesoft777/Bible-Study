# Hungarian Károli Bible (1590)

## Description

The Károli Bible is the first complete Hungarian translation of the Bible, prepared by Gáspár Károli (c. 1529-1591), a Hungarian Reformed pastor. Published in Vizsoly in 1590, it was translated from the original Hebrew and Greek. The Károli Bible is the Hungarian equivalent of the King James Version — it shaped the Hungarian literary language and remains the classic Hungarian Protestant Bible. It includes the Protestant canon (66 books).
